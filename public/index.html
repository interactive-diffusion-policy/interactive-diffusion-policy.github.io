Abstract

Interactive Diffusion Policy (IDP) separates reactive avoidance from task resumption. Link-mounted capacitive proximity sensors feed RMPflow to adjust the robot's motion near obstacles. When avoidance takes the robot outside its demonstration distribution, Latent Policy Barrier (LPB) guides the diffusion policy back toward demonstrated states. LPB uses dynamics trained on expert demonstrations and real avoidance-and-recovery rollouts, with operator corrections collected when needed.
